# Weight & Balance

A simple weight and balance calculator for light training aircraft. It runs fully in your browser.

**Try it online:** https://rocketflash-net.github.io/weight-balance/

## Features

- **Built-in aircraft:** Cessna 152, Cessna 172S, and Piper PA-28-161 Warrior II, with typical POH data. Cessna 208B Grand Caravan is included with **estimated** data (Jet A fuel) that still needs checking against the POH.
- **Full load sheet:** zero fuel, ramp, takeoff (after taxi fuel), and landing (after fuel burn), each with weight, CG, and moment.
- **CG envelope chart:** shows your takeoff, landing, and zero fuel points. The C172S also shows the Utility category.
- **Automatic checks:** max weights, CG limits, baggage limits, and fuel capacity.
- **Custom aircraft:** copy a built-in aircraft and edit everything (stations, weight limits, envelope) to match your own POH.
- **Units:** pounds and US gallons, or kilograms and liters.
- **Languages:** English and Indonesian.
- Your inputs and custom aircraft are saved in your browser.

## Running locally

No installation or build step needed. Download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser.

## Important

The built-in numbers are examples from a typical POH. Every aircraft has its own empty weight and arm, and limits can differ between models and years. Always use the POH/AFM and the W&B record of the aircraft you will fly. This app is for practice and cross-checking, not an official document.
