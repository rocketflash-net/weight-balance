    Cara pakai

    1. Pilih pesawat, lalu isi berat kosong (BEW) dan arm-nya dari W&B record pesawat kamu.
    2. Isi berat pilot, penumpang, bagasi, dan bahan bakar.
    3. Isi bahan bakar untuk taxi dan yang terpakai selama terbang.
    4. Pastikan titik takeoff dan landing ada di dalam envelope, dan semua cek berwarna hijau.

    Rumus dasar

    • Momen = Berat × Arm
    • CG = Total momen ÷ Total berat
    • Avgas dihitung 6 lb/gal (0,72 kg/L).
    • CG bergeser saat bahan bakar terbakar. Karena itu cek CG di takeoff dan di landing, juga CG tanpa bahan bakar (zero fuel).

    Penting

    Angka pesawat bawaan adalah contoh dari POH tipikal. Setiap pesawat punya berat kosong dan arm sendiri, dan batasnya bisa berbeda antar model atau tahun. Selalu gunakan POH/AFM dan W&B record pesawat yang akan diterbangkan. Aplikasi ini untuk latihan dan pengecekan silang, bukan dokumen resmi.

    How to use

    1. Pick an aircraft, then enter the basic empty weight (BEW) and its arm from your aircraft's W&B record.
    2. Enter the pilot, passenger, baggage, and fuel weights.
    3. Enter the taxi fuel and the fuel you plan to burn in flight.
    4. Make sure the takeoff and landing points are inside the envelope and every check is green.

    The basics

    • Moment = Weight × Arm
    • CG = Total moment ÷ Total weight
    • Avgas is counted as 6 lb/gal (0.72 kg/L).
    • The CG moves as fuel burns, so check it at takeoff, at landing, and with no fuel (zero fuel).

    Important

    The built-in aircraft numbers are examples from a typical POH. Every aircraft has its own empty weight and arm, and limits can differ between models and years. Always use the POH/AFM and W&B record of the aircraft you will fly. This app is for practice and cross-checking, not an official document.
